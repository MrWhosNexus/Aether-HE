AETHER HE - BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Aether HE is free, open-source software (GPL-3.0-or-later) made by an
independent community. It is NOT made, endorsed or supported by Aula or any
keyboard manufacturer. Product names are used only to say which hardware it
works with.

NO WARRANTY. Aether writes settings (lighting, actuation, Rapid Trigger, SOCD,
key remaps, macros, calibration) to your keyboard. It is provided "as is",
without any warranty, and the authors are not liable for damage to your
keyboard, lost settings, lost warranty or any other loss (GPL-3.0 sections 15
and 16). Boards marked "bring-up" or "experimental" have not been verified on
real hardware. Aether never flashes firmware.

GAMES AND COMPETITIONS. SOCD / Snap Tap behaviour, Rapid Trigger, macros, key
remaps and the virtual gamepad are banned or restricted in some games,
leagues and tournaments. Checking and following those rules is your
responsibility.

PRIVACY. Aether runs locally. No account, telemetry, analytics or AI service.
Key statistics are per-key counts only, never what you type, and can be turned
off. The app only goes online to check GitHub for updates and, for game
detection, to fetch Discord's public list of game names. Details: PRIVACY.md.

AI. Much of Aether was written with AI assistance and reviewed by a human
maintainer. No AI runs inside the app. Details: AI_DISCLOSURE.md.

These documents are installed in the "legal" folder next to the app and are
published at https://github.com/MrWhosNexus/Aether-HE
